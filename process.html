My Workflow

From Idea to Website

A simple process I can follow when creating websites and digital projects.

01

Discover

Understand the purpose, audience, requirements, and goals of the project.

02

Plan

Organize the content, features, page structure, and development tasks.

03

Design

Create a clean visual direction, layout, colors, typography, and user experience.

04

Develop

Build the website using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and other appropriate technologies.

05

Test

Check responsiveness, links, buttons, forms, layout, and overall usability.

06

Deliver

Polish the final project, prepare the files, and make the website ready to use.
